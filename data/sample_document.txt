Remote Work and Flexible Benefits Policy

Section 1: Remote Work Eligibility
All full-time employees are eligible to work remotely up to five days a week, subject to
manager approval. Employees must be reachable during core hours, which are 10:00 AM to
4:00 PM in their local time zone. New hires must complete a 30-day in-office onboarding
period before switching to a remote schedule.

Section 2: Health Insurance
Employees become eligible for health insurance coverage after 90 days of continuous
employment. The company covers 100% of the employee's premium and 50% of dependents'
premiums. Coverage includes dental and vision as standard, with no additional opt-in
required.

Section 3: Learning and Development Stipend
Every employee receives an annual learning stipend of INR 15,000 to spend on courses,
books, certifications, or conference tickets. Unused stipend does not roll over to the
following year. Reimbursement requests must be submitted within 60 days of purchase.

Section 4: Paid Time Off
Employees accrue 1.5 days of paid time off per month, capped at 18 days per year.
Unused PTO up to 5 days may be carried over into the next calendar year. There is no
cap on sick leave, but absences longer than 3 consecutive days require a manager's
sign-off.

Section 5: Equipment and Home Office Allowance
New employees receive a one-time home office allowance of INR 20,000 to cover a desk,
chair, monitor, or other equipment. Laptops are provided directly by the company and
remain company property.

Section 6: Performance Reviews
Performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in April and October. Ratings use a
five-point scale, and compensation adjustments tied to reviews take effect the month
following the review cycle.

Section 7: Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers
are entitled to 4 weeks of paid parental leave. Leave must be taken within 12 months of
the child's birth or adoption.
